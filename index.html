System requirements

Frequently asked questions

Consensus, custody, fees and node requirements — answered without the marketing gloss.

Choice Coin is an ASA on Algorand, which uses Pure Proof of Stake. Blocks are final the moment they are written — there are no forks to wait out and no reorg risk, so a settled vote stays settled. Median finality across recent governance rounds is roughly 2.4 seconds.
The ballot and tally contracts are open source and reviewed before each protocol release. Every deployed contract address is published with its source, so you can diff what is running against what was reviewed rather than taking our word for it.
A vote is a single Algorand transaction, so the network fee is a fraction of a cent — currently 0.001 ALGO. Choice Coin adds nothing on top for voters on any plan, including the free tier.
MyAlgo Wallet is supported today, with Pera and WalletConnect sessions rolling out. Connections are non-custodial: the app requests a signature per ballot and never holds keys, seed phrases or funds.
Nothing, unless you want to. Voting works entirely through public infrastructure. Organizations that need their own indexer can point Choice Coin at a private node on the Custom Node plan — 4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM and an SSD are comfortable for an archival setup.
